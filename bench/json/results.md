# json adapter benchmark

Run on 2026-09-24 with `jev-1.13.0`, 9 tasks over two synthetic datasets (`bench/json/`, generated by `make_data.py`), 3 runs per task and arm.

| Arm | Accuracy | Median input tokens | Median latency (ms) | Median confidence | Median options |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| raw | 89% (24/27) | 3848 | 573 | 1.00 | 36 |
| jevbrief | 100% (27/27) | 2002 | 612 | 1.00 | 17 |

| Task | raw | jevbrief |
|---|---|---|
| tickets: a customer was billed twice this month | 3/3 | 3/3 |
| tickets: someone keeps getting sent back to the sign-in page | 3/3 | 3/3 |
| tickets: a big customer says the app is down | 3/3 | 3/3 |
| tickets: refund for a damaged order | 3/3 | 3/3 |
| tickets: the spreadsheet export is not working | 0/3 | 3/3 |
| tickets: take away access for someone who left the company | 3/3 | 3/3 |
| catalog: something to keep me dry on a rainy hike | 3/3 | 3/3 |
| catalog: a light shelter for two people backpacking | 3/3 | 3/3 |
| catalog: warm socks for long walks | 3/3 | 3/3 |

## Notes

- The raw arm sends every item with every field. The jevbrief arm uses the config in `tickets.toml` or `catalog.toml`.
- The raw arm's only miss: for "the spreadsheet export is not working" it picked a stale 90-day-old look-alike ticket, which the `stale` rule drops.
- The datasets are small and synthetic, and the look-alike tickets were written to test the rules. Treat the accuracy gap as an illustration, not a general result.
- Input tokens are the real `usage.input_tokens` reported by the API.
